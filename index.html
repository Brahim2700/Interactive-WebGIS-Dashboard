About the distortion estimate

This is a simplified educational estimate: 3.1 × (latitude − parallel of origin)². It depends mainly on latitude, not longitude. The actual Lambert projection has zero scale distortion on its standard parallels (±0.75° from the origin); this simplified estimate does not model that behavior.

For contractual, cadastral, or high-precision calculations, use the official IGN algorithms.
